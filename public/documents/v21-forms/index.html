Appendix E: Sample Gate Raffle Tickets

Appendix E: Sample Gate Raffle Tickets

GATE CONTROL ONLY—NOT A BALLOT

BLUE = Ahiazu Mbaise | GREEN = Ezinihitte Mbaise | YELLOW = Aboh Mbaise

Illustrative examples of ordinary commercially available numbered raffle tickets. No special MPU printing is required. The color and ticket number are used only for gate control, LGA identification, and reconciliation.

BLUE - Ahiazu Mbaise

GREEN - Ezinihitte Mbaise

YELLOW - Aboh Mbaise

Gate use: After eligibility and LGA verification, the gate clerk issues the appropriate color-coded numbered raffle ticket and records the ticket number next to the member's name. The voter is marked present at the gate.

Voting-table use: The voter surrenders the raffle ticket and signs the Voter Register before receiving the corresponding official ballot. Surrendered tickets are retained for reconciliation. Ticket numbers must never be written on, attached to, or otherwise linked to a voter's marked ballot.

The raffle ticket is not a voting ballot.
