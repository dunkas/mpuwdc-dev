Handwritten / Plain-Paper Submission

When an official printed form is not readily available, a member or candidate may use a clean sheet of plain paper. The submission is accepted when all required information for the applicable election action is clearly provided and the document is signed and dated.

IDENTITY
Full Name & LGA
ELECTION ACTION
Office / Position or Purpose
CONTACT
Email & Phone / WhatsApp
DATE
Date of Submission
CERTIFICATION
Signature Required
IF REQUIRED
Profile / Supporting Information
What the signature means: By signing and dating the submission, the member certifies that the information provided is true to the best of their knowledge, accepts the stated nomination or candidacy where applicable, confirms willingness to serve if elected, and acknowledges that eligibility will be verified by the Electoral Committee using the MPU Constitution and official MPU records. The signature fulfills the declaration/acknowledgment requirement for the signed plain-paper submission; it does not by itself establish eligibility.

Photo / Scan Submission

A clear photograph or scan of a completed and signed official form or plain-paper submission may be sent through an approved electronic channel.

  • Show the entire page — do not crop the name, date or signature.
  • Make sure handwriting is readable and lighting is clear.
  • The sender must be identifiable through the approved email, WhatsApp, portal or other authorized channel.
  • The record must be capable of being retained and printed by the Electoral Committee.
  • The submission must be received by the applicable published deadline.
Important: A photo or scan is only a way of transmitting the signed written record. It does not remove any required information, signature, deadline or eligibility-verification requirement.
