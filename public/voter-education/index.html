Signature certification: By signing and dating the submission, the member certifies that the information provided is true to the best of their knowledge, accepts the stated nomination or candidacy where applicable, confirms willingness to serve if elected, and acknowledges that constitutional eligibility will be independently verified by the Electoral Committee using official MPU records.
